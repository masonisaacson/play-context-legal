Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 12, 2026

About this policy

This policy explains information handled when you use Play Context and its services. It concerns you as an app user; public sports information about teams and athletes is part of the app’s content.

Information on your device

The app saves followed teams, onboarding progress, viewing preferences, and cached game information on your device. It does not require a separate Play Context account. Searches within the app filter the available team or player information locally.

Followed teams can affect the team schedules requested from our servers. Keeping a preference on your device does not mean that all use of that preference stays offline. Device backups may also include saved app data, depending on your Apple settings.

Game requests and service logs

When you load schedules, games, players, team information, or news, the app sends the relevant dates and sports identifiers to our service. Our hosting infrastructure receives connection information such as an IP address. Service logs can include requested paths, request identifiers, times, response status, and processing duration.

This information supports delivery of the content, troubleshooting, and protection against excessive or unauthorized requests. The backend is hosted using Railway.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes payments and manages your Apple Account subscription. Play Context does not receive your full payment-card details. The app reads Apple-verified purchase information to check access and restore subscriptions. Signed purchase evidence may be sent to our service to verify paid access.

RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription information, including transaction identifiers, products, purchase and expiration dates, and renewal or refund status. It associates this information with a generated app-user identifier and uses it to support subscription tracking and reporting. This identifier does not require you to provide a name or email address to Play Context. RevenueCat also receives Apple’s vendor identifier for your device when available, which is different from Apple’s advertising identifier, along with technical information such as app and system versions, locale, and currency.

Images, news, and other services

Some team logos load from third-party content servers, including CFBD and ESPN content hosts. Those servers receive the image request and connection information, such as an IP address. Sports data is also obtained by our backend from CFBD and ESPN.

Opening an article takes you to its publisher’s website. That site can process information under its own privacy policy. Apple and RevenueCat also describe their handling of information in their policies, linked below.

Our public support and legal pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives connection information when you visit those pages and handles it under its privacy statement. Those pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts, or contact forms. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, and use them to respond and handle your request. Our contact address uses SimpleLogin to forward messages to our support inbox; SimpleLogin and our email provider process those messages to deliver them.

Permissions and tracking

The app does not ask for your contacts, camera, microphone, photo library, or precise device location. It does not include an advertising SDK or use Apple’s advertising identifier. Play Context does not itself track your activity over time across other companies’ websites or apps.

Other parties may collect personal information about your online activity over time and across different websites or apps when your device loads their content or you open their sites from Play Context. For example, ESPN’s parent company, Disney, describes combining activity information and using it for personalization and advertising across sites and apps. This does not mean every logo request is used that way; those providers’ policies and your settings govern their practices.

Our app and public-page code do not respond to browser Do Not Track signals. Hosting providers and external websites may respond under their own policies. Our public pages contain no advertising or tracking scripts. You can use your browser’s privacy controls when visiting external sites and consult their policies for available choices.

Your choices

You can change followed teams in Settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data from that installation, but does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete records held by Apple, RevenueCat, hosting services, or device backups.

Manage subscription renewal in your Apple Account settings. Apple provides controls for its account data and backups. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, subject to applicable exceptions. Contact us using the address below to make a request or ask a question about your information.

Describe your request and the information or activity it concerns. We may ask for limited information needed to locate relevant records and verify that you are entitled to make the request. An email address alone may not identify an anonymous subscription record. Do not send Apple Account passwords, full payment-card details, or government identification with your initial request.

We handle requests manually using our records and service providers’ supported tools. We will explain the outcome and any applicable limits, including records we cannot identify or must retain. Deleting subscription records does not cancel your Apple subscription. Continued use, restoring a purchase, or making a new purchase can create records again.

How long information is kept

Local preferences and caches remain on your device until changed, removed, or replaced by the app or operating system.

We keep operational records for as long as needed to deliver the service, troubleshoot problems, and protect its security. We keep support correspondence to resolve your request and provide relevant follow-up. Subscription records are kept as needed to manage access, refunds, disputes, and applicable legal obligations.

When those purposes no longer require personal information we control, we delete or de-identify it, subject to applicable legal, security, and dispute-related exceptions. For information held by service providers, we use their available tools or request their assistance. Provider-controlled logs, backups, and legally required records may remain under the providers’ retention practices; removal from active records does not necessarily erase every backup immediately. Apple maintains its account and transaction records under its own policies and legal requirements.

Children and policy changes

Play Context is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us using the address below so we can review the concern and take appropriate action.

This page will show an updated date when the policy changes. Material changes will be explained with the updated policy and any additional notice or choice required by law.
